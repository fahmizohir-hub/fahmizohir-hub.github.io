Privacy Policy: Fahmi Zohir Reels

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Who uses this app

Fahmi Zohir Reels is a private tool used only by the owner of the Facebook Page "Fahmi Zohir" to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and TikTok account. It is not offered to other users and does not collect data from the public.

What Google / YouTube data we access

The app requests one Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload, which allows it to upload videos to the signed-in owner's YouTube channel. It also uses the YouTube Data API with an API key to read public video titles and view counts for topic research. It does not read, download or analyse any private YouTube or Google account data.

What TikTok data we access

The app uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API with the scopes user.info.basic (to show which account is connected) and video.upload (to send the owner's own videos to their TikTok inbox as drafts). It does not read other users' data, messages, followers or analytics, and it never posts publicly on its own; the owner finishes each post in the TikTok app.

How the data is used and stored

Access tokens issued by Google and TikTok are stored only on the owner's private server and are used only to upload the owner's own videos with their caption. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Third-party terms

Use of this app is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, the Google Privacy Policy, the TikTok Terms of Service and the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Google access can be revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions. TikTok access can be revoked in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions. When access is revoked, the stored token stops working and is deleted from the server.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a message to the Fahmi Zohir Facebook Page.
