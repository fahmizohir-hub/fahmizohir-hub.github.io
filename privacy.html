Privacy Policy: Fahmi Zohir Reels

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Who uses this app

Fahmi Zohir Reels is a private tool used only by the owner of the Facebook Page "Fahmi Zohir" to upload the Page's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to other users.

What Google data we access

The app requests one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload, which allows it to upload videos to the signed-in user's YouTube channel. It does not read, download or analyse any other YouTube or Google account data.

How the data is used and stored

The OAuth token issued by Google is stored only on the owner's private server and is used only to upload the Page's own videos with their title and description. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

YouTube Terms and Google Privacy Policy

By using this app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When access is revoked, the stored token stops working and is deleted from the server.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a message to the Fahmi Zohir Facebook Page.
