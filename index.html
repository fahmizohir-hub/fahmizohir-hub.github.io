Fahmi Zohir Reels

Short educational videos by "Fahmi Zohir".

Fahmi Zohir Reels is a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the Fahmi Zohir Facebook Page. It takes the owner's own short educational videos (world facts, "Why...?" and "What happens if...?" explainers made with low-poly 3D animation and Malay narration) and uploads them to the owner's own accounts:

  • YouTube channel (as Shorts)
  • TikTok account (sent to the TikTok inbox as a draft; the owner reviews and posts it in the TikTok app)
This tool is not offered to the public. Only the account owner signs in, and only to upload their own videos to their own accounts.
