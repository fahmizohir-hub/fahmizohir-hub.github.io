Fahmi Zohir Reels

Short world-fact videos by the Facebook Page "Fahmi Zohir".

Fahmi Zohir Reels is a private publishing tool used only by the owner of the Fahmi Zohir Facebook Page. It uploads the Page's own short educational videos ("Kenapa ...?" world-fact explainers) to the owner's own YouTube channel.

This tool is not offered to the public. Only the channel owner signs in, and only to upload videos to their own channel.
